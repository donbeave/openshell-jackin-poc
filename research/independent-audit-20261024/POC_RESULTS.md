This is not native Linux or Docker Desktop evidence. Source baseline is Jackin `6e266406...`, Architect `7b72b38...`. Images and target UUIDs/generations are recorded in raw per-experiment records. Current OpenShell main is source-only review, never mixed into these successes. Client/gateway binary hashes are in `evidence/binary-identities.json`. Exact trusted runtime/supervisor image digests were not retained by the control fixture; those infrastructure-image attribution fields remain NOT TESTED rather than reconstructed from current cache tags.

## Reproduction and ownership

Read each POC's README and script before running. Gateway configuration/state/certificates are scoped under that POC, on distinct ports 18817/18818/18819 with distinct sandbox labels; global gateway selection and default namespace are not changed. Generate new certificates; old private keys are not evidence artifacts. Resource names are POC-owned, never foreign `jfr24` or other active runs. Docker operations for image building and OpenShell infrastructure are distinct from workloads executed through OpenShell.

Historical role direct-Docker hook/version diagnostic logs are explicitly excluded (`evidence/role/excluded-docker-probes.md`); replay is disabled. They cannot establish OpenShell hook/version compatibility and do not remain in the proposed replacement path. This was caught by independent review.

Capsule actual cleanup polled its authorized scope and namespace empty, removed gateway state/generated keys, and stopped only its own gateway processes. The control teardown initially inferred stop/port facts without captured commands; its original claims are preserved and marked invalid in `gateway-teardown.json`. Parent read-only `evidence/final-scoped-observation.json` independently observes no listeners on 18817/18818/18819 and empty exact owned container/volume namespaces now; it does not establish the earlier stopping actor. The replay script was corrected to record intent/IDs and confirm asynchronous deletion; its correction received syntax validation but no new live replay. Control/worker final receipts are `evidence/control/final-cleanup.json`, `worker-group-cleanup.json` and `gateway-teardown.json`; namespace/UUID-scoped containers and volumes were empty, own state/config/generated private keys removed. Whole-sandbox deletion confirms compute removal without yielding a worker process result. Role cleanup receipt records one unused own image-tag removal plus 584,827,248 bytes of downloaded binaries removed after URLs/checksums were preserved. Derived warm replay then failed because its COPY input had been cleaned; it is not a cache PASS. Concurrent foreign inventory changes are observed, not attributed without evidence. No unrelated mutation command is authorized by the reset allowance.

## Measurement limits

New POCs do not form a performance benchmark. Cache reuse includes shared BuildKit/public base layers; no cold-host claim or tail percentile. Prior measurements keep their original n/cache definitions. Time to usable authenticated agent, three-domain aggregate cost, required VM/remote/Kubernetes overhead, combined Docker/child budget, concurrent startup and same-hardware current-pin Jackin comparison remain NOT TESTED. Functional failures must be fixed before a meaningful full-architecture cost comparison.

Final independent static source signoff covers 207 canonical direct records, 10 cross-cutting records, 19 infrastructure records and 1,266 reconciled broad matches. See `audits/INDEPENDENT_AUDITS.md`. Final current observations after all replay attempts are in `evidence/final-scoped-observation-after-replay.json`; all four owned namespaces had no containers/volumes and all four POC ports had no observed listener. These are current scoped observations, not proof of unchanged unrelated inventory. Final cleanup details and recovery map are in `evidence/disk-keeper.md`.
